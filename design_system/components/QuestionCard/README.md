# QuestionCard

The agent's question: up to four options, the expected gain against the 5.26% break-even, the cost on every answer, and a free Skip that changes nothing.

- **Provide:** `q` (`text`, `options`, `gain`, `breakEven`, `cost`, `wordedBy`, `scope` breadcrumb, `answer`), `evidence`, optional `state` (`open` | `answered` | `skipped`), `cueLine`, `hoverKey`, `number`, `brief` (stage: no points or links).
- Keyboard 1–4 and S. Copy-all-links and QR for teammates' devices; nothing seen there goes back in except a click on an option.
- The card never says "skip if…".
